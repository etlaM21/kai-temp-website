title: Description
---

The Residenztheater München presents a radical reimagining of Peter Handke’s Kaspar by integrating a Large Language Model directly into the live rehearsal and performance process. In this digital-age laboratory, the AI acts as the modern prompter, subjecting the protagonist to a visceral cycle of algorithmic speech torture and synthetic socialization. This production marks a haunting collision between avant-garde theater and 21st-century code, questioning what remains of the human soul when our very thoughts are shaped by predictive patterns.
